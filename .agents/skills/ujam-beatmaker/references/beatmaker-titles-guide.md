# UJAM Beatmaker - Titles & Special Controls Guide

Each UJAM Beatmaker plugin is curated for a specific genre, featuring custom drum kits, styles, mix presets, and a unique **Special Slider** tailored to the genre's mixing and production demands.

## Master Processing Section (Common to All Titles)

Beyond the genre-specific Special Slider, all Beatmakers share a powerful 4-parameter Master Section:

1. **Sweep**:
   - Bipolar dual-filter control.
   - **Center (0%)**: Filter bypassed.
   - **Turn Left (< 0%)**: Resonant Low-Pass Filter (LPF) sweep — cuts highs, ideal for intros and breakdowns.
   - **Turn Right (> 0%)**: High-Pass Filter (HPF) sweep — thins out low end, ideal for pre-drop tension builds.

2. **Saturate**:
   - One-knob analog tape / tube saturation.
   - Low settings add warm subtle harmonics; high settings create aggressive pumping distortion.

3. **Maximize**:
   - Perceptual loudness maximizer that boosts density and punch without causing digital clipping overs.

4. **Ambience & Ambience Select**:
   - 6 switchable reverb/space algorithms per plugin (from tight studio rooms to vast halls).
   - Ambience knob controls dry/wet send amount.
