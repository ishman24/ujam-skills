## 2. Bass Guitar Setup Section (Physical Tone)

### A. Pickup Blend
- Continuous knob blending between two custom pickup configurations:
  - **Neck Position**: Warmer, rounder, slightly hollow, and more "guitar-like" timbre.
  - **Bridge Position**: Tight, nasal, punchy, rich in mid harmonics and attack.
  - **Center**: Balanced full-spectrum tone.

### B. Character Switch
A 4-way chicken-head switch that recalibrates the frequency curve and dynamic spectrum of the instrument:

| Setting | Tonal Profile | Dynamic Range | Recommended Production Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Soft** | Full low-end, attenuated mids and highs | Soft to Mid | Ballads, slow soul, ambient tracks leaving huge space for vocals. |
| **Warm** | Emphasized low-end, warm slight mid boost | Full | Standard go-to setting for pop, rock, and modern radio tracks. |
| **Neutral**| Direct flat response | Full | Best when feeding external amp simulators (AmpliTube, Neural DSP). |
| **Hard** | Attenuated sub lows, aggressively boosted high mids | Mid to High | Driving rock riffs, fast licks, melodic bass solos, pick bite. |

### C. Drop D Switch
- When engaged, physically tunes the lowest string down a whole step from **E to D** (down to MIDI note 26).
- Extends the bottom range to 36.7 Hz for heavy rock, drop-D riffs, or modern modern pop sub impact.

## 3. Amplifier & Drive Section

Four amplifier models providing distinct cabinet, speaker, and tube saturation stages:

| Amp Setting | Virtual Rig & Speaker Hardware | Sonic Color | Ideal Genre |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rock** | American vintage tube head + 8x10 "fridge" cab | Punchy, aggressive, massive midrange thrust | Pop/Rock, Alternative, Hard Rock |
| **Soul** | Warm tube head + 1x15" speaker + high-frequency tweeter | Hollow, deep, warm, smooth vintage vintage | Motown, R&B, Soul, Classic Disco |
| **Pop** | British transistor head + 4x10" cab | Clean, hi-fi, linear, tight transient definition | Modern Pop, Funk, Electronic, Dance |
| **Direct** | Pure DI (Direct Injection) signal bypass | Completely clean, uncolored direct bass signal | External amp sims or ultra-clean acoustic bass |

### Drive Knob
- Controls preamp overdrive saturation into the chosen Amp model.
- Adds tube grit, dynamic compression, and rich harmonic overtones (bypassed in Direct mode).

## 4. Bass Channel Audio Processing

Three purpose-built processing modules pre-calibrated for bass guitar:

### A. Compressor
- One-knob macro compressor. Smooths out dynamics and boosts perceived loudness.
- At maximum settings, activates a **transient shaper** that accentuates the initial finger pluck or pick strike.

### B. Equalizer (4-Band Parametric Macro)
Continuous morphing control tailored to bass frequencies:
- **Vintage (< Neutral)**: Big sub-bass boost, scooped upper-mids and attenuated treble.
- **Neutral (12 o'clock)**: Flat, balanced response.
- **Modern (> Neutral)**: Scooped lower mids, boosted punch around 80 Hz, and sparkling treble top-end for slap or pick definition.

### C. Octaver
- Generates a synthetic octave-up duplicate of the bass signal.
- Dialed subtly: adds melodic presence, harmonic cut, and bite when driving amps.
- Dialed high: creates prominent synth-bass or 8-string bass effects ideal for high fills and melodic solos.

## 5. Finisher Multi-Effects Section

Introduced in Version 2, the Finisher delivers 15 bespoke multi-effect algorithms combining distortion, chorus, flanger, envelope filters, delays, and ambient reverbs.

- **Finisher Knob**: Automation-ready macro controlling the intensity and wet/dry depth of the active effect.
- Automating the Finisher knob during pre-choruses or transitions creates swelling, psychedelic, or gated bass sweeps.
